## 01. 01 QS 可持续排名：UNSW 升到全球第 5

**一句话**：11 月 17 日 UNSW 官网发布：在 QS Sustainability Rankings 2027 中，UNSW 上升两位到全球第 5，继续排澳洲第一。

## 02. 02 郝晓静教授获 Hamakawa Award

**一句话**：11 月 18 日 UNSW 官网发布：Scientia Professor 郝晓静（Xiaojing Hao）获得世界光伏能源转换大会的 Hamakawa Award。

11 月 18 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW Scientia Professor Xiaojing Hao is the first Australian photovoltaics scientist to receive the Hamakawa Award in recognition of her breakthroughs in developing the next generation of solar energy cells.

Hamakawa Award 由世界光伏能源转换大会颁发，表彰在太阳能转换方面有突出研究和技术贡献的科学家与工程师。郝教授 2006 年以博士生身份进入光伏领域，至今已把四种不同的薄膜光伏技术做到世界纪录效率。

她接下来的目标是推进硅基叠层太阳能电池。她说：We need curious, creative and ambitious students who want to tackle challenges that matter.

> 来源：[UNSW Newsroom · 2026-11-18](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/11/unsw-scientia-professor-xiaojing-hao-wins-prestigious-engineering-award)

## 03. 03 Wagga Wagga 首届全程医学生毕业

**一句话**：11 月 20 日 UNSW 官网发布：第一批在 Wagga Wagga 乡村临床校区完成全部双学位医学培训的学生毕业。

11 月 20 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW has celebrated a regional education milestone, with the graduation of the first cohort of medical students to do their entire dual-degree training at the UNSW Rural Clinical Campus in Wagga Wagga.

UNSW Medicine & Health 从 2021 年起在 Wagga Wagga 提供完整的双学位医学课程。官方原文：The 15-strong Class of 2026 includes 11 students with rural backgrounds.

Ten graduates are set to commence their internships in regional Australia next year, including five locally at Wagga Wagga Base Hospital. Wagga Wagga 是 UNSW 第一个乡村临床校区，也是 2000 年建立的澳洲第一所 Rural Clinical School 所在地。
