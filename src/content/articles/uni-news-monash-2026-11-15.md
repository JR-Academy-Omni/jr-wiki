## 02. 02 器官衰老研究：Granzyme K 与心肝肾衰老有关

**一句话**：11 月 13 日 Monash 官网发布：Monash Biomedicine Discovery Institute 团队在 Immunity 期刊发表研究，发现 Granzyme K 与器官衰老损伤有关。

11 月 13 日，Monash 官网发布：Published in Immunity, the research discovered a link between the protein Granzyme K and signs of ageing in the heart, liver and kidneys.

Granzyme K 是免疫细胞产生的一种蛋白，官方原文：Granzyme K is a protein produced by immune cells, with levels known to increase significantly with age in both mice and humans.
