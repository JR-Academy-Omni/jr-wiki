## 1. 新州设立 1.5 亿澳元科学与创新基金

9 月 11 日，UNSW Newsroom 报道。官方原文：**Announced by NSW Treasurer Daniel Mookhey at the Sydney Investment Summit this week, the fund will co-invest with NSW public universities before expanding to other organisations across the innovation ecosystem**。

基金定位官方说明为：**The fund forms part of a new framework designed to improve the pathway from research to commercialisation and support investment in areas including technology, biomedical science and environmental science**。

UNSW 校监 Warwick Negus AM 表示欢迎该笔战略投资。对关注科技转化与创业方向的同学，这类州级基金落地后通常会衍生新的实习、孵化与产学合作机会，但具体名额与申请方式需以后续官方公告为准。

> Source: [UNSW Newsroom · New $150 million fund to boost research and innovation in NSW](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/09/new-150-million-fund-to-boost-research-and-innovation-in-nsw)

## 2. ARC Future Fellowships：28 位学者、3280 万澳元

UNSW Newsroom 公布，本轮 ARC 2026 Future Fellowships 有 28 位 UNSW 学者获资助，合计 3280 万澳元。全澳层面官方原文：**The Future Fellowships scheme provides more than $344 million in funding for over 290 research projects across Australia**。

项目例子：**Dr Hamid Alinejad Rokny received $1,167,038 to develop OmniSB, an artificial intelligence platform that analyses genetic and cellular data to map tissue structures, identify cell interactions and predict biological responses**。

Future Fellowships 属多年期资助，获资助课题组在未来几年经费相对稳定，招收研究生与提供 stipend 的空间通常更大。

> Source: [UNSW Newsroom · UNSW experts awarded $32.8 million to drive ambitious research](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/09/unsw-experts-awarded--32-million-to-drive-ambitious-research)

## 3. 三个 NHMRC 卓越研究中心落地

9 月 7 日，UNSW Newsroom 报道。官方原文：**UNSW researchers have been awarded $9 million in government funding for research into dementia, autoimmune disease and the prevention of epidemics in aged care**。

三个中心分别为：血管性痴呆卓越研究中心（Prof. Perminder Sachdev，UNSW Medicine & Health），聚焦血管性认知障碍与痴呆的诊断、治疗与预防；PEACCCC 养老机构疫情防控中心（Prof. Raina MacIntyre，Kirby Institute），聚焦呼吸道与肠胃道疫情的感染防控；自身免疫病体细胞突变与治疗中心（Prof. Christopher Goodnow，Garvan Institute 与 UNSW Medicine & Health），通过靶向携带 DNA 突变的细胞理解和治疗自身免疫病。

每个中心获 300 万澳元，属于联邦本轮向全澳 22 个新中心投入的 6600 万澳元的一部分。

> Source: [UNSW Newsroom · UNSW awarded $9 million for three new Centres of Research Excellence](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/09/unsw-awarded-9-million-for-three-new-centres-of-research-excellence)
