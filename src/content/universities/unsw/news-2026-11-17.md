## 1. AFR 2026 Best Universities：UNSW 从第 3 升到第 1

11 月 16 日，UNSW 官网发布消息：在 Australian Financial Review 的 2026 Best Universities 排名中，UNSW 总排名从第 3 升到第 1。这个排名从教学、科研、职业影响和公平四个维度比较澳洲大学。

官方原文：For career impact, the University scored 99 out of 100, putting it 10 points ahead of the second-ranked university. In the remaining pillars, UNSW ranked third for research, seventh in teaching and 18th in equity.

Career Impact 指标参考 QILT 毕业生调查（毕业一年和三年后）、雇主满意度、全职就业和薪资数据，UNSW 连续四年在这一项排名第 1。

## 2. 2027 John Monash Scholarship：UNSW 校友入选 18 人名单

11 月 13 日，UNSW 官网宣布校友、UNSW Medicine & Health 兼职讲师 Sambavan Jeyakumar 博士入选 18 名 2027 John Monash Scholars，获 Tim Fischer John Monash Scholarship，计划在 Imperial College London 读 Master of Research in Medical Robotics and Image-Guided Intervention。

官方原文：The prestigious postgraduate scholarships are valued at $100,000 a year for up to three years of study and are awarded annually following a highly competitive nationwide selection process.

Jeyakumar 博士目前是 Royal Prince Alfred Hospital 的外科住院医生，研究方向是用机器人技术改善澳洲偏远地区获得专科手术的机会。

> Source: [UNSW Newsroom · 2026-11-13](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/11/unsw-academic-wins-2027-john-monash-scholarship)

## 3. 三个项目获 2026 Engagement Australia Awards

11 月 12 日，UNSW 官网宣布三个项目获 2026 Engagement Australia Awards，覆盖原住民健康、太平洋地区太阳能维修和海洋保护。今年奖项收到 121 份申请，分 8 个类别。

官方原文：In Vanuatu, the program has trained 141 people across eight islands, helping establish repair businesses and improve access to solar repair services for more than 93,000 people.

Kirby Institute 的 First Nations Molecular Point-of-Care Testing Program 获 Indigenous Engagement 奖，支持六个司法辖区 90 多家原住民社区健康机构，覆盖九种传染病检测。
