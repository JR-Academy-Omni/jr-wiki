## 1. QS 可持续排名：UNSW 升到全球第 5

![QS 可持续排名：UNSW 升到全球第 5](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/unsw-wide/general/buildings-facilities/2026-10-19-general-campus/2026-10-29-UNSW-General-Campus-SH02-0006.cropimg.width=700.crop=landscape.jpg)

11 月 17 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW Sydney has risen two places to fifth globally in the 2027 QS Sustainability Rankings, retaining its position as Australia’s top-ranked university.

官方原文：The University’s overall score increased from 98.2 in 2026 to 99.0. 其中 Social Impact 并列全球第 2。

这个榜今年是第五届，用 50 多项指标评估 Environmental Impact、Social Impact 和 Governance。Almost 6000 universities were assessed, with results published for 2003 institutions.

> Source: [UNSW Newsroom · UNSW ranks first in Australia and fifth globally in QS Sustainability Rankings](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/11/unsw-ranks-first-in-australia-and-fifth-globally-in-qs-sustainability-rankings)

## 2. 郝晓静教授获光伏领域 Hamakawa Award

![郝晓静教授获 Hamakawa Award](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/engineering/general/websites/2024-11-martin-green-project/2024-12-xiaojing-hao-profile.cropimg.width=700.crop=landscape.jpg)

11 月 18 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW Scientia Professor Xiaojing Hao is the first Australian photovoltaics scientist to receive the Hamakawa Award in recognition of her breakthroughs in developing the next generation of solar energy cells.

官网介绍，郝教授 2006 年以博士生身份进入光伏领域，至今已把四种不同的薄膜光伏技术做到世界纪录效率。她接下来的方向是硅基叠层太阳能电池。

她在采访里说：We need curious, creative and ambitious students who want to tackle challenges that matter.

> Source: [UNSW Newsroom · UNSW Scientia Professor Xiaojing Hao wins prestigious engineering award](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/11/unsw-scientia-professor-xiaojing-hao-wins-prestigious-engineering-award)

## 3. Wagga Wagga 校区首届全程医学生毕业

![Wagga Wagga 首届全程医学生毕业](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/unsw-wide/general/news/2026/2026-11-unsw_grad-2026_mattbeaver_dsc09931_final.cropimg.width=700.crop=landscape.jpg)

11 月 20 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW has celebrated a regional education milestone, with the graduation of the first cohort of medical students to do their entire dual-degree training at the UNSW Rural Clinical Campus in Wagga Wagga.

UNSW Medicine & Health 从 2021 年起在 Wagga Wagga 提供完整的双学位医学课程。官方原文：The 15-strong Class of 2026 includes 11 students with rural backgrounds.

Ten graduates are set to commence their internships in regional Australia next year, including five locally at Wagga Wagga Base Hospital.

> Source: [UNSW Newsroom · First UNSW students complete full medical program at Wagga Wagga campus](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/11/first-unsw-students-complete-full-medical-program-at-wagga-wagga-campus)
