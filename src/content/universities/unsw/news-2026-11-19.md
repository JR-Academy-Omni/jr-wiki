## 1. QS 可持续排名：UNSW 升到全球第 5

![QS 可持续排名：UNSW 升到全球第 5](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/unsw-wide/general/buildings-facilities/2026-10-19-general-campus/2026-10-29-UNSW-General-Campus-SH02-0006.cropimg.width=700.crop=landscape.jpg)

11 月 17 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW Sydney has risen two places to fifth globally in the 2027 QS Sustainability Rankings, retaining its position as Australia's top-ranked university.

总分从 2026 年的 98.2 升到 99.0。分项上，Social Impact 并列全球第 2，Environmental Impact 第 11（上升 7 位），Governance 并列第 44；Social Impact 下的 Employability and Opportunities 排全球第 14。

这一届近 6,000 所大学参评，公布 2,003 所结果。校长 Attila Brungs 教授：Sustainability is embedded across UNSW's strategy and culture.

## 2. 郝晓菁教授获光伏领域 Hamakawa Award

![郝晓菁教授获光伏领域 Hamakawa Award](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/engineering/general/websites/2024-11-martin-green-project/2024-12-xiaojing-hao-profile.cropimg.width=700.crop=landscape.jpg)

11 月 18 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW Scientia Professor Xiaojing Hao is the first Australian photovoltaics scientist to receive the Hamakawa Award in recognition of her breakthroughs in developing the next generation of solar energy cells.

Hamakawa Award 由 World Conference on Photovoltaic Energy Conversion 于 2014 年设立，不是每年都颁发。郝教授 2006 年作为博士生进入光伏领域，已把 4 种不同的薄膜光伏技术做到世界纪录效率，提名人是 Scientia Professor Martin Green。

她鼓励学生入行：We need curious, creative and ambitious students who want to tackle challenges that matter.
