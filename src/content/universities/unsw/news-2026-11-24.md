## 1. UNSW 成立公共政策研究生院，首批公开课程 2027 年开课

![UNSW Graduate School of Public Policy](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/law/general/websites/2026-09-impact-stories/2026-09-defending-democratic-constitutionalism.cropimg.width=700.crop=landscape.jpg)

11 月 23 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW 成立 UNSW Graduate School of Public Policy，面向公务员、政治幕僚、议员，以及非营利和私营部门的政策从业者。学院设在 UNSW 新的 Canberra City Campus。

课程内容覆盖预算与立法流程、采购、大型公共服务和地缘政治，也包括 AI 治理、网络风险、能源转型和财政取舍等技术议题。学院执行院长为 Rebecca Razavi，顾问委员会由前新州部长 Rob Stokes 担任主席，成员包括 Productivity Commission 主席 Danielle Wood。

关于课程安排，官网原文：The School is already delivering tailored programs for government agencies. The first public courses begin in 2027. It is developing executive education, professional certificates, a Graduate Certificate focused on data and evidence in policymaking, and an Executive Master for strategic public leadership, with short courses designed to stack into longer qualifications.

## 2. 理论物理学家 Victor Flambaum 获 2027 Norman F. Ramsey Prize

![Victor Flambaum](https://www.unsw.edu.au/content/dam/images/science/physics/2026-11-victor-flambaum/2026-11-victor-flambaum.cropimg.width=700.crop=landscape.jpg)

11 月 19 日，UNSW 官网发布：UNSW Sydney Scientia Professor Victor Flambaum has been named the winner of the 2027 Norman F. Ramsey Prize for seminal contributions to physics.

该奖由美国物理学会（American Physical Society）每年颁发，表彰原子、分子与光物理以及基本定律和对称性精密检验方面的工作。官网写到，他是该奖 2017 年设立以来获奖的首位澳洲研究者。

Flambaum 教授的研究涉及基本常数变化、基本对称性破缺，以及用于下一代 GPS 和自动驾驶系统的超精密离子钟与核钟。他在官网表示：It is rewarding that experiments based on our ideas and calculations have been carried out in more than 50 laboratories around the world.
