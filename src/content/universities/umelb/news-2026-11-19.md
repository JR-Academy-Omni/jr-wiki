## 1. QS 可持续排名：墨大从第 11 升到第 9

![QS 可持续排名：墨大从第 11 升到第 9](https://www.unimelb.edu.au/__data/assets/image/0004/5666647/Dookie-Field-Research-0768.jpg)

11 月 17 日，墨大官网发布 QS World University Rankings: Sustainability 2027 结果。官方原文：Overall, the University rose from 11th last year to 9th in the latest rankings, achieving an overall score of 97.9 out of 100.

Social Impact 一项墨大排全球第一。官方原文：The University of Melbourne also ranked first in the world in the Social Impact category, achieving the maximum possible score of 100.

这个榜评估全球近 6,000 所大学，52 项指标分 Environmental Impact（45%）、Social Impact（45%）和 Governance（10%）三类。另有 6 所澳洲大学进入全球前 50。墨大还把可持续内容放进了 3,000 多名大一化学学生的课程。

## 2. 四校新闻系学生将直播维州大选之夜

![四校新闻系学生将直播维州大选之夜](https://www.unimelb.edu.au/__data/assets/image/0009/5666418/MEDIALAB-DSCF1050-1.jpg)

11 月 17 日，墨大官网发布：Journalism students from four Victorian universities will deliver live radio coverage of the Victorian State Election, reporting election results and expert analysis to audiences across the state.

项目由墨大 Centre for Advancing Journalism and Information Integrity（CAJII）牵头，墨大、RMIT、Swinburne、La Trobe 的学生在墨大 Parkville 新的 Media Production Studios 组成联合新闻室，和资深记者、政治专家一起工作。

CAJII 主任 Andrea Carson 教授：By working alongside experienced journalists and political experts, students will develop the skills, accuracy and judgement needed to deliver trustworthy information to the public in real time. 直播 11 月 28 日（周六）晚 6 点在 Vision Australia Radio 开始。

> Source: [UniMelb Newsroom · Student journalists from four universities to lead live Victorian election broadcast](https://www.unimelb.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/november/student-journalists-from-four-universities-to-lead-live-victorian-election-broadcast)
