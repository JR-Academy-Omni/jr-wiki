## 1. COP31 解读：190 多个国家齐聚土耳其

11 月 9 日，UQ 官网刊出 Matt McDonald 教授的分析文章。官方原文：This week in Turkiye, more than 190 countries will come together to discuss global action on addressing climate change.

这届大会由土耳其主办，澳洲由气候变化部长 Chris Bowen 担任谈判主席。官方原文：The headline goal of COP31 is a target associated with electrification.

文章也讨论了美国退出 UNFCCC 留下的领导力空缺。官方原文：The US contributes at least 12 per cent of global emissions.

## 2. 托儿中心餐食报告：小镇只有 58% 提供

11 月 11 日，UQ 官网发布：UQ 与 Social Ventures Australia 合作的报告 Food where it matters most，首次在全国范围梳理 0 到 6 岁儿童长托服务的餐食供应。

官方原文：The most significant gaps were found in small rural towns where only 58 per cent of services provided food.

研究者 Bonnie Searle 博士指出：Between 16 and 43 per cent of Australian households with children experience food insecurity。官方原文：At the same time, more than 1 in 6 Australian children live in poverty.
