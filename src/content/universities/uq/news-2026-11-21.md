## 1. QS 可持续排名升 22 位

11 月 19 日，UQ 官网发布：The University of Queensland (UQ) has risen 22 places to rank 28th globally in the QS Sustainability Rankings.

分项上，官网原文是：UQ ranked equal first for health and wellbeing impact and second nationally for environmental research. 这个榜单从 environmental impact、social impact 和 governance 三个维度评估大学。

这是可持续专项榜，不是综合排名。选校时可以当一个参考维度，专业和课程设置还是要去官网逐项看。

## 2. 残奥泳将的 Churchill 访学

![残奥泳将的 Churchill 访学](https://news.uq.edu.au/sites/default/files/styles/uqds_card/public/2026-11/hmbs-rachael-watson-churchill-fellowship-headline.jpg)

11 月 18 日，UQ 官网发布专题 The Paralympian paving the way for more champions in the pool。残奥游泳运动员 Rachael Watson 的目标是 2028 年在洛杉矶拿到第三枚金牌，她刚以 Churchill Fellow 身份在欧洲待了 3 个月，现在回到了布里斯班。

她在文中说：It was a huge challenge finding a coach who wanted to train an athlete with a disability. 官网还写到，The Winston Churchill Memorial Trust has awarded more than 4,500 fellowships since 1965.

Watson 认为现行的残奥分级体系可能让一部分游泳运动员处于不利位置。她说：Since the Paralympics began in 1960, Australia has not been represented by swimmers in particular classifications.
