## 1. AFR 2026 Best University Ranking：UQ 总排名第 3

![UQ Great Court](https://news.uq.edu.au/sites/default/files/styles/card_image/public/2025-05/great-court-with-students-walking.jpg?h=3dbd9eac&itok=zBH2pNrk)

11 月 16 日，UQ 官网发布：在 Australian Financial Review 2026 Best University Ranking 中，UQ 总排名第 3，教学全国第 2。

这个排名从 research、teaching、career impact、equality 四个维度评估澳洲大学，数据来自公开来源，包括 QS World University Rankings 和 Academic Ranking of World Universities。

官方原文：The University of Queensland (UQ) has ranked third overall and placed second nationally for teaching in the Australian Financial Review’s (AFR) 2026 Best University Ranking.

## 2. UQ 与奥克兰大学签 5 年国际合作协议

![UQ 与奥克兰大学签约](https://news.uq.edu.au/sites/default/files/styles/card_image/public/2026-11/atsis-auckland-agreement-te-kawehau-with-bronwyn-fredericks-1080w.jpg?h=a81fead4&itok=gMD4Qxzq)

11 月 17 日，UQ 官网宣布：UQ 与 Waipapa Taumata Rau, University of Auckland 签署新的 International Cooperation Agreement。两校自 2000 年起保持机构合作。

协议将支持原住民研究、原住民教育与教学、原住民治理交流等方向的后续合作。

官方原文：The 5-year agreement builds on more than 2 decades of institutional partnership between the universities.

> Source: [UQ News · 2026-11-17](https://news.uq.edu.au/2026-11-uq-strengthens-indigenous-partnership-new-zealand)

## 3. 12 米 CLT 天花板实火测试

![木结构实火测试](https://news.uq.edu.au/sites/default/files/styles/card_image/public/2026-11/drupal-and-v6.jpg?h=4bb2700c&itok=VodPUCK_)

11 月 12 日，UQ 官网发布：UQ 消防安全工程团队与 Queensland Fire Department 合作，对交叉层压木（CLT）天花板隔间做实火测试，研究建筑设计如何影响火势。每个隔间内部用直播摄像头和热量、压力传感器监测。

该研究是 PhD 候选人 Josh Madden 的课题，属于 UQ 土木工程学院管理的 ARC Research Hub to Advance Timber for Australia’s Future Built Environment。

官方原文：The research team constructed 12-metre long cross-laminated timber (CLT) ceiling compartments and set them alight under the supervision of QFD firefighters at their training ground at the Port of Brisbane.

> Source: [UQ News · 2026-11-12](https://news.uq.edu.au/2026-11-improving-safety-timber-buildings-setting-them-fire)
