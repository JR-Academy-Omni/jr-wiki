## 1. 校长赴京出席第 9 届中澳 CEO 圆桌，提出扩大双向学生流动

![校长赴京出席中澳 CEO 圆桌](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0004/4491679/VC-at-Australia-China.jpg)

11 月 21 日，Monash 官网发布：Monash University Vice-Chancellor and President, Professor Sharon Pickering, has joined business and government leaders in Beijing for the 9th Australia-China CEO Roundtable, held alongside Prime Minister Anthony Albanese’s official visit to China.

圆桌由 Business Council of Australia 和 China Development Bank 主办。校长在会上提了两项教育政策建议：建立定期的中澳教育部长级对话；以及 I also called for increased government funding to support the expansion of two-way student mobility experiences between Australia and China.

她还介绍了 Monash 与宝武钢铁（Baowu Steel）、中国钢铁（China Steel Corporation）合作的低排放绿色钢铁研究，以及绿色氨合成、电池级锂提取、航空轻量电机和下一代储能等项目，并提到在人工智能治理方面与中方合作。

## 2. MIPS 与 Leica 共建 M-LAB 生物成像中心

![MIPS 与 Leica 共建 M-LAB](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0011/4487789/Enyuan-Cao-Intestinal-villi-showing-drug-uptake.jpg)

11 月 17 日，Monash 官网发布：The Monash Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences (MIPS) and Leica Microsystems have today established a new partnership to advance bioimaging research, innovation and scientific discovery.

中心全称 MIPS-Leica Advanced Bioimaging（M-LAB），设在 Parkville 校区。官方原文：The upgraded facility provides superior speed, enhanced resolution to 30 nanometres, and a dramatic expansion in colour capacity – jumping from six colours up to 16 distinct channels.

新设备还支持活体成像，可以实时追踪整个生物系统里的代谢过程和分子相互作用。MIPS 所长 Chris Porter 教授表示，成像对研究所各方向的研究越来越关键，合作能帮助团队在接近分子的精度上理解药物和递药系统的转运与沉积。

> Source: [Monash Newsroom · Monash and Leica partner to create M-LAB, new centre for imaging excellence](https://www.monash.edu/newsroom/news/2026/monash-and-leica-partner-to-create-m-lab,-new-centre-for-imaging-excellence)
