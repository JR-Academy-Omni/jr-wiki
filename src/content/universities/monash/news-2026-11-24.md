## 1. Monash 牵头的 CREST Hub 启动，研究锂、硅等关键材料分离技术

![CREST Hub 膜中试设施](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0005/4491257/Facility-photo-05-1.jpg)

11 月 20 日，Monash 官网发布：The ARC Research Hub for Critical Energy Separation Materials (CREST Hub), officially launched today, bringing together five Australian universities and six industry partners to develop technologies that could make resource recovery more efficient, reduce energy consumption, and strengthen Australia's position in emerging clean energy industries.

中心由 Monash 牵头，主任是 Matthew Hill 教授，并纳入 Monash 与 CSIRO 联合的 Membrane Pilot Facility，用来把实验室技术放到接近真实工况下测试和放大。研究方向包括从复杂矿石和回收废料中分离有价值材料的膜技术、金属提取用的环保溶剂、氨的安全运输材料，以及把二氧化碳转化为氢运输材料的工艺；其中一个重点是低碳生产硅金属，这种材料用于太阳能板、手机、电动车、电池和 AI 基础设施。

对研究生来说，官网写明：Since commencing in June 2026, the CREST Hub has established four research projects, appointed its first research staff and enrolled four PhD students. A further nine PhD students are expected to commence in 2027, alongside growth in its research workforce.

## 2. 26 位 Monash 学者入选 Clarivate 2026 高被引学者名单

![Monash 高被引学者](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0012/4476378/media-release-thumbnail-3-2.jpg)

11 月 19 日，Monash 官网发布：Twenty-six Monash University academics have been recognised for their global research influence as part of Clarivate’s 2026 list of Highly Cited (HiCi) Researchers.

今年 Monash 新增 3 位入选者，其中包括通过 EMERGE @ Monash 人才计划从纽约加入理学院的 Benjamin Cook 教授。入选学者的方向覆盖可持续发展、神经心理学、心血管疾病、女性生殖健康、地球演化、电化学和天体物理。信息技术学院的 AI 研究者 Geoff Webb 教授今年首次入选。

副校长 Robyn Ward 教授在官网表示：It’s fantastic to see such a broad range of disciplines represented, with seven of our 10 faculties featuring on the list.
