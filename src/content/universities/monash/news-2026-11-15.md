## 1. 两名 Monash 本科生获 Order of Australia Association Foundation Scholarship

![两名 Monash 本科生获 Order of Australia Association Foundation Scholarship](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0007/4484365/Order-of-Aus-Assoc-Award-group-photo-1.jpg)

11 月 12 日，Monash 官网发布：本科生 Lara Abboud 和 Jesse Wrobel 获得 Order of Australia Association Foundation Scholarship。官方原文：They are among six undergraduate second-year award students recipients chosen from across Australian universities, with each student receiving $45,000 in financial support, as well as mentorship from a member of the Order of Australia.

官方介绍，这个奖学金 recognises academic excellence alongside extensive contribution to their community，由总督 Sam Mostyn AC 在堪培拉总督府宣布。

Lara 就读 Bachelor of Education (Honours) 中学教育和 Bachelor of Arts（历史）；Jesse 就读 Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) 和 Bachelor of Commerce。这条新闻是获奖公告，没有写申请资格和申请方式，想了解的同学请以基金会官网为准。

## 2. Granzyme K：Monash 发现推动器官衰老的蛋白

![Granzyme K：Monash 发现推动器官衰老的蛋白](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0005/4485362/La-Gruta-Immunity-WT-and-GzmK-deficient-kidney-images-1.jpg)

11 月 13 日，Monash 官网发布：Monash Biomedicine Discovery Institute 团队在 Immunity 期刊发表研究。官方原文：Published in Immunity, the research discovered a link between the protein Granzyme K and signs of ageing in the heart, liver and kidneys.

Granzyme K 由免疫细胞产生，官方原文：levels known to increase significantly with age in both mice and humans, but its precise role in age-related organ damage has remained unclear.

Nicole La Gruta 教授表示，没有这种蛋白的小鼠器官衰老迹象减少，而免疫系统照常衰老，这挑战了器官衰老与免疫衰老必然绑定的假设。研究使用的是临床前小鼠模型。

> Source: [Monash News · Monash scientists identify protein driving age-related organ damage](https://www.monash.edu/news/articles/monash-scientists-identify-protein-driving-age-related-organ-damage)
