## 1. Monash 连续第三年被评为澳洲研究第一的大学

![Monash 研究排名](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0009/4494177/FXB1901228-1.jpg)

11 月 25 日，Monash 官网发布：Monash University has outperformed every other Australian university and been titled Australia's top institution for researchers and leading research fields in The Australian's annual list of the country's top researchers and research institutions.

这已经是连续第三年。官网原文：Monash was named the top research institution in 43 out of the 250 fields, with 33 Monash researchers named as the top Australian researchers in their field.

另外，Monash 还入选 Translation Readiness Index 评出的澳洲 25 家最具发明力研究机构。Victorian Heart Hospital 负责人 Stephen Nicholls 教授和 Monash 商学院的一位教授入选澳洲 20 位「Superstars of Research」。这份 Top 250 Researchers 榜单从 2018 年开始，依据研究者过去五年在顶级期刊上获得的引用数据。

## 2. Monash 商学院报告：一半购物者计划参与黑五

![黑五购物](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/image/0008/4494410/GettyImages-636613962-2.jpg)

11 月 25 日，Monash 商学院 Australian Consumer and Retail Studies（ACRS）发布 Black Friday Spotlight 报告。官网原文：Planned participation in Black Friday sales has risen from 33 per cent of shoppers in 2022 to 50 per cent in 2026, while planned Boxing Day participation is 29 per cent in 2026, down six percentage points from last year.

报告显示，70% 的受访者计划在今年的促销活动中购物（2025 年为 65%）。参与原因里，49% 是为了折扣，47% 是为了不付原价，只有 28% 是因为喜欢逛。

品类方面，53% 的促销购物者计划买服装鞋履配饰，35% 计划买美妆个护；消费电子下降 13 个百分点至 25%，美妆个护是唯一增长的品类。渠道上实体店最受欢迎（67%），其次是 Myer、JB Hi-Fi 这类实体零售商的官网（55%）。

> Source: [Monash Newsroom · Black Friday cements itself as Australia's top sales event](https://www.monash.edu/newsroom/news/2026/black-friday-cements-itself-as-australias-top-sales-event)
