## 1. Karen Canfell 获州长癌症研究奖年度最高荣誉

![Canfell 获州长癌症研究奖](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/corporate/images/news-and-opinion/news/2026/november/nsw-premiers-awards-for-outstanding-cancer-research---award-winners-pictures-with-prof-tracey-obrien-am-and-susan-pearce-am.jpg)

11 月 20 日，悉大官网发布：流行病学家 Karen Canfell AC 教授获 2026 NSW Premier's Awards for Outstanding Cancer Research 的最高荣誉 Premier's Outstanding Cancer Researcher of the Year，奖项由 Cancer Institute of NSW 联合主办。

官方原文：Few Australians have made such a significant contribution to cancer research and prevention as Professor Karen Canfell.

Canfell 教授在 Sydney School of Public Health 负责 Cancer Elimination Collaboration，研究影响了澳洲和国际上的宫颈癌预防与筛查政策，也包括 HPV 筛查和弱势群体的公平可及。她的获奖感言：This award is incredibly meaningful to me and our team.

## 2. 实验性滴鼻剂在小鼠身上改善学习和记忆

![滴鼻剂改善小鼠记忆](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/corporate/images/news-and-opinion/news/2026/november/dr-maria-bergamasco-in-the-dr-john-and-anne-chong-lab-for-functional-genomics,-charles-perkins-centre.-photo-stefanie-zingsheim-university-of-sydney.jpg)

11 月 19 日，悉大官网发布：Charles Perkins Centre 的 Greg Neely 教授团队开发了一种实验性滴鼻剂，经鼻腔把 brain-derived neurotrophic factor（BDNF）的 mRNA 送入大脑。

官方原文：The researchers found nose drops improved learning and memory in aged mice. 另一句原文：Reduced BDNF levels are associated with ageing and Alzheimer’s disease.

研究中这种方法没有减少淀粉样斑块，因此可能与现有抗淀粉样疗法互补。论文发表在 Advanced Science，目前仍是临床前研究。

> Source: [University of Sydney News · 2026-11-19](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/11/19/experimental-nose-drops-tested-in-mice-could-prevent-alzheimer-s.html)
