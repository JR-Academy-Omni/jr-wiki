## 1. QS Sustainability 2027：悉尼大学全球第 17

![悉尼大学 Quadrangle](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/campus-facilities-and-amenities/campus-landscapes/drone-quadrangle.jpeg)

11 月 17 日，悉尼大学官网发布：在 QS World University Rankings: Sustainability 2027 中排名全球第 17。榜单从 environmental impact、social impact、governance 三个类别评估。

悉尼大学 social impact 类别全球第 7；其中 employability & opportunities 第 20（上升 8 位），equality 第 22（上升 42 位）。

官方原文：The QS rankings feature 2003 institutions across 107 locations internationally, including 39 institutions in Australia.

> Source: [University of Sydney News · 2026-11-17](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/11/17/sydney-17th-globally-in-qs-rankings-for-sustainability-.html)

## 2. Hatch Students：为在读学生创造带薪岗位

![悉尼大学学生](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/people/students/group-of-female-students-studying-at-new-engineering-building.jpg)

11 月 17 日，悉尼大学官网宣布：与 Deloitte 和 Hatch 合作推出全国试点 Hatch Students，为悉尼大学学生创造配合学业安排的带薪工作。Hatch 的 AI 按技能、价值观和准备程度匹配学生，并作为 employer of record 处理雇佣事务。

官方原文：Major Australian employers already committed to the pilot include Deloitte, Westpac, Bupa, AGL, RoadTek (Queensland Department of Transport and Main Roads) and Flight Centre Travel Group.

官网这篇没有写国际学生参与条件和每周工时，请向学校和 Hatch 官方核对，并对照学生签证的工作条件。

> Source: [University of Sydney News · 2026-11-17](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/11/17/university-of-sydney-deloitte-and-hatch-create-new-category-of-student-work.html)

## 3. 生物医学转化 Academy：每个项目 $100,000

![Sydney Biomedical Accelerator](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/corporate/images/news-and-opinion/news/2026/november/nick-hunt-endo-axiom-whiteboard.jpeg)

11 月 12 日，悉尼大学官网宣布：Sydney Biomedical Accelerator 与 Proto Axiom 合作推出 Academy。每个项目团队推荐一名 Academy Fellow（通常为博士后），从 2027 年初起每周约一天在 Proto Axiom 及其创投网络中工作。

官方原文：Over 12 months, up to three emerging life sciences ventures from the University of Sydney or Sydney Local Health District will receive hands on training and entrepreneurial support, as well as $100,000 each.

> Source: [University of Sydney News · 2026-11-12](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/11/12/australian-first-academy-to-fast-track-biomedical-discoveries.html)
