## 1. USYD 学生紧急补助最高 $2,000，求助学生 3 年增长超 60%

![USYD 学生 Jasmine](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/corporate/images/news-and-opinion/news/2026/november/jasmine-and-mother-2.jpg)

11 月 24 日，USYD 官网发布学生 Jasmine 的故事。她因意外两个月无法工作，申请到 Student Bursaries Fund 的补助后，钱几天内到账，她用来交清学费，还剩一些买日用品。

官网原文：The Student Bursaries Fund provides emergency financial assistance of up to $2,000, helping students overcome immediate challenges that might otherwise interrupt their education.

官网同时写到：In just three years, the number of students seeking financial assistance from the University's Student Life team has increased by more than 60 percent, with around 150 students now reaching out every month. 以及 Since 2021, the cost of being a student in Australia has risen by 29 percent, outpacing inflation across the broader economy. 学校预计 2027 年需要发放更多补助，尤其在每学期开学时。原文没有写申请条件以及国际学生是否适用，请以学校 Student Life 官方页面为准。

> Source: [USYD News · A helping hand when Jasmine needed it most](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/11/24/a-helping-hand-when-jasmine-needed-it-most.html)

## 2. USYD 学者获 2026 总理科学奖

![2026 总理科学奖](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/corporate/images/news-and-opinion/news/2026/december/georginafengwang.jpeg)

11 月 24 日，USYD 官网发布 2026 Prime Minister’s Prizes for Science 获奖消息。官网原文：The University is the only institution to have more than one recipient named this year.

Georgina Long 教授获 Prime Minister’s Prize for Science，官网称她的黑色素瘤研究 improved survival tenfold for people with stage IV melanoma and 24-fold for those with brain metastases.

另一位 USYD 研究者获 2026 Malcolm McIntosh Prize for Physical Scientist of the Year，研究方向是碳捕集：His creation of a new acidic reactor system to prevent this loss converts industrial CO2 emissions into ethylene.
