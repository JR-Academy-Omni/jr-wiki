## 1. 全校学生和教职工免费用 ChatGPT Edu

9 月 14 日，悉尼大学官网发布。官方原文：**All University of Sydney students and staff will soon gain free and supported access to OpenAI's ChatGPT Edu, the enterprise AI tool of choice for a number of leading universities worldwide**。

开放节奏官方也写清楚了：**ChatGPT Edu features will be introduced in stages, with additional capabilities being progressively enabled throughout the rollout**——功能分阶段上线，能力逐步开放。官网未给逐项时间表，具体开通以学校通知为准。

配套设立 AI Sustainability Research and Innovation Fund，官方描述为 **strategic seeding investment of $1 million**，用于理解、衡量并降低 AI 的环境影响。学校同时明确会在维护学术诚信、保护知识产权、负责任使用与隐私的前提下支持师生建立 AI 能力。

> Source: [The University of Sydney · All students and staff to receive free and supported access to ChatGPT Edu](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/09/14/students-staff-to-receive-free-chatgpt-edu-access.html)

## 2. 口服胰岛素进入人体试验

9 月 10 日，悉尼大学官网：学校衍生公司 Endo Axiom 启动口服胰岛素的 Phase 1a 临床研究。官方原文：**This Phase 1a clinical study marks Endo Axiom, a spinout from the University, as a clinical-stage company**。

试验细节：**first-in-human dosing of 12 participants**，随机、双盲、安慰剂对照，在阿德莱德的 CMAX Clinical Research 开展。下一步官方说明为 **A phase 1b trial in people with type 1 diabetes will follow**。

团队来自医学与健康学部，包括副教授 Nicholas Hunt、教授 Victoria Cogger、教授 David Le Couteur。公司成立于 2023 年，聚焦多肽、蛋白、抗原与 RNA 的纳米递送平台。

> Source: [The University of Sydney · Oral insulin drug achieves a critical milestone](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/09/10/oral-insulin-drug-achieves-a-critical-milestone--.html)

## 3. ARC Future Fellowships 拿下 39 个项目

9 月 9 日，悉尼大学官网公布本轮 ARC Future Fellowships 结果。官方原文：**University of Sydney Fellows received more than $45 million to lead these ambitious projects over the next four years**，共 39 个项目。

全澳层面：**$344 million awarded to 290 outstanding mid-career researchers across the country**。覆盖学科官方列出 science, engineering, health, humanities, social sciences, architecture and law。项目例子之一是 Dr Conrad Wasko 改进洪水风险理解与预测方法的研究。

Future Fellowships 为四年期资助，获资助课题组在未来几年经费相对稳定，招收研究生的意愿通常更强。找导师时可把该名单作为第一层筛选。

> Source: [The University of Sydney · University of Sydney leads Australia in ARC Future Fellowships](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/09/09/university-of-sydney-leads-australia-in-arc-future-fellowships.html)
