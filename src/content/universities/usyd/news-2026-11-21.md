## 1. 28 位学者入选高被引名单

![28 位学者入选高被引名单](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/intranet/images/news/2023/quad-tower-through-cloisters.jpg)

11 月 18 日，悉尼大学官网发布：Twenty-eight researchers from the University of Sydney have been named among the world's most influential scientists.

官网介绍这份年度名单的标准：The annual list identifies researchers whose publications rank in the top one percent by citations in their fields.

Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Research) Professor Mike Ryan 在文中说：Research impact is built over many years through curiosity, dedication and collaboration. 打算申研究型学位的同学，可以去官网原文看入选学者，再对照自己的方向找导师。

## 2. 医学毕业生回乡村带教

![医学毕业生回乡村带教](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/corporate/images/news-and-opinion/news/2026/november/joe-duncan.jpg)

11 月 18 日，悉尼大学官网发布 From winemaking to rural practice: medical grad now training next generation of country doctors。官网写道：Dr Joe Duncan first came to the region as a Sydney medical graduate and basic physician trainee. 文中还有一句：Joe's path to medicine was far from conventional.

他在文中说：The huge benefit is I offer the same level of service that people would get in Sydney.

文章发布时正值 UCRH 25 周年。Head of the Rural Clinical School 的 Professor Vicki Flood 说：Joe's career offers a living example of that legacy: student becomes doctor, doctor becomes teacher, and the cycle continues.
