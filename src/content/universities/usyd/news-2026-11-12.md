## 1. NSW 警方模拟训练向大学生开放

11 月 6 日，悉尼大学官网发布消息：NSW 警方的模拟训练向大学生开放。官方原文：The simulation involved 35 students from a range of disciplines.

参加的学生来自法律、社工、心理、医学、文科和数据科学。这次模拟是一个学期的 Industry and Community Project Unit 的收尾环节。

官方原文：Open to students from all degrees, it combined lectures from industry experts with practical experience through realistic scenarios. 官网同时写明：The program will continue next year.

> Source: [University of Sydney News · 2026-11-06](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/11/06/nsw-police-open-simulation-training-to-university-students.html)

## 2. AI 时代怎么教、怎么考：两条车道

11 月 6 日，悉尼大学官网刊出副校长（教学）Adam Bridgeman 的专访。他的原话：The key shift we're making is to move from restricting AI use to developing student judgement and critical engagement.

考核采用两条车道的做法。官方原文：One lane uses supervised, in-person assessments where AI use is controlled. 另一条车道的考核允许使用 AI。

文中提到悉大的 Cogniti：Approximately 5,000 agents are currently in use in schools and universities around the world. 文章还提到一份新的绿皮书 Education in the Age of AI。

> Source: [University of Sydney News · 2026-11-06](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/11/06/from-learning-to-leadership.html)
