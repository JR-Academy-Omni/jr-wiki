## 1. ReconNECKt 颈痛试验：12 周免费远程治疗

11 月 12 日，阿德莱德大学官网发布：阿大牵头全国试验 ReconNECKt，比较两种慢性颈痛的治疗方式，参与者跟踪 12 个月。

官方原文：Participants will receive 12 weeks of free treatment from an allied health clinician via telehealth, including personalised advice, education and exercises they can complete at home.

资格条件，官方原文：To be eligible for the study, participants must be aged 18 or over, have experienced neck pain for at least three months and have pain that was not caused by an accident or significant injury.

文中引用的背景数据：World Health Organization data shows that approximately 1.71 billion people globally live with musculoskeletal conditions, with 222 million people living with chronic neck pain.

## 2. Helen Marshall 获任南澳首席科学家

11 月 12 日，阿德莱德大学官网发布人事消息。官方原文：Renowned Adelaide University medical researcher Professor Helen Marshall AM has been appointed South Australia's new Chief Scientist for a three-year term, commencing on 1 March 2027.

官方原文：As incoming Chief Scientist, Professor Marshall will provide independent advice to the South Australian Government on matters relating to science, technology, engineering and mathematics, research and innovation.

她将接替 Craig Simmons 教授。
