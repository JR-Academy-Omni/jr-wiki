## 1. 虚假信息感知影响民主信任

![虚假信息感知影响民主信任](https://media.cdn.adelaide.edu.au/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:ebc9a1da-457d-449e-95f0-226c4baaf361/as/DisinfoMobile.jpg?quality=80&auto-format=true)

11 月 20 日，Adelaide University 官网发布研究新闻 Misinformation perception shapes support of democracy。研究者请 123 名有投票资格的选民追踪自己感知到的虚假信息接触情况。

官网写到，超过 50% 的参与者表示看到过自己认为是虚假信息的内容。一作 Dr Natasha van Antwerpen 说：Misinformation, both verified and perceived, was associated with lower institutional trust and democratic satisfaction.

共同作者 Dr Toby Prike 说：We should be examining misinformation's relationships with broader attitudes including institutional trust. 读心理学、传播学、政治学的同学可以去官网看全文。

## 2. 蝙蝠传播疾病与气候变化

![蝙蝠传播疾病与气候变化](https://media.cdn.adelaide.edu.au/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:b4b89f65-c5ed-48d3-81e6-a35e1c6e76b5/as/Bat-mobile.jpg?quality=80&auto-format=true)

11 月 19 日，Adelaide University 官网发布 Climate change and habitat loss could fuel deadly bat-borne disease。研究者梳理了一批危险的蝙蝠传播疾病的传播、预防和管理。

一作、School of Medicine 的 Dr Jessica Stanhope 说：Climate change and habitat loss are putting increasing pressure on bat populations around the world.

School of Animal and Veterinary Sciences 的 Associate Professor Wayne Boardman 说：Extreme heat places enormous stress on bats, potentially increasing the amount of virus they shed. School of Public Health 的 Emeritus Professor Philip Weinstein 提到，Adelaide is currently losing mature trees to urban development.
