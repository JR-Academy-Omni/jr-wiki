01 Student Bursaries Fund：最高 $2,000 紧急补助
一句话：11 月 24 日 USYD 官网发布学生 Jasmine 的故事，介绍由捐赠支持的 Student Bursaries Fund。

11 月 24 日，USYD 官网发布学生 Jasmine 的故事。她因意外两个月无法工作，申请到补助后 the money arriving in her account within days，用来交清学费并买日用品。

官网原文：The Student Bursaries Fund provides emergency financial assistance of up to $2,000, helping students overcome immediate challenges that might otherwise interrupt their education.

02 USYD 学者获 2026 总理科学奖
一句话：11 月 24 日 USYD 官网发布：多位 USYD 研究者在 2026 Prime Minister’s Prizes for Science 中获奖。

11 月 24 日，USYD 官网发布 2026 Prime Minister’s Prizes for Science 获奖消息。官网原文：The University is the only institution to have more than one recipient named this year.

Georgina Long 教授获 Prime Minister’s Prize for Science，官网称她的黑色素瘤研究 improved survival tenfold for people with stage IV melanoma and 24-fold for those with brain metastases.

另一位获奖者获 2026 Malcolm McIntosh Prize for Physical Scientist of the Year，研究方向是碳捕集：His creation of a new acidic reactor system to prevent this loss converts industrial CO2 emissions into ethylene.
